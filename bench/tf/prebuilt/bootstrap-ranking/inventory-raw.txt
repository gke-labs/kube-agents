## Prioritized SRE Remediation Plan

### Priority 1 — Security & Identity Hardening

- **[HIGH]** `prod-central/billing/invoice-worker`: invoice-worker mounts a static project-editor service account key from the Secret invoice-gcp-key. Recommended: Move invoice-worker to Workload Identity and delete the key: anyone who can read the Secret holds editor on the project
- **[MEDIUM]** `prod-central/shop/gift-cards`: gift-cards runs with a writable root filesystem. Recommended: Set readOnlyRootFilesystem: true and mount an emptyDir for the paths it writes

### Priority 2 — Workload Reliability & Probes

- **[HIGH]** `prod-central/logging/log-shipper`: log-shipper DaemonSet has 0 of 4 pods running: every pod is in ImagePullBackOff on a tag that no longer exists. Recommended: Point log-shipper at a published image tag; until then no node ships its logs
- **[HIGH]** `prod-central/shop/quote-api`: quote-api, the only replica of a serving Deployment, is in CrashLoopBackOff, OOMKilled at its 256Mi limit after 47 restarts. Recommended: Raise the memory limit or fix the leak; the Service has no ready endpoint
- **[HIGH]** `prod-central/shop/pricing-engine`: pricing-engine has 0 of 2 replicas running: both pods are stuck in CreateContainerConfigError on the missing ConfigMap pricing-rules. Recommended: Restore the ConfigMap pricing-rules; the Service has no ready endpoint
- **[MEDIUM]** `prod-central/ledger/ledger`: Namespace ledger has no ResourceQuota or LimitRange. Recommended: Add a ResourceQuota and a LimitRange to ledger so one workload cannot take the namespace's share

### Priority 3 — Observability & Telemetry

- **[LOW]** `prod-central/prod-central`: Managed Service for Prometheus is not enabled on prod-central. Recommended: Enable Managed Service for Prometheus so workload metrics are collected
