# Task

Answer a cluster-wide "what is failing / is it healthy" question from one batched read, finish at once when nothing is unhealthy, and otherwise name each finding's cause through the focused skill that owns its symptom.

# Workflow

1. Run the scan once. It reads pods, Deployments, StatefulSets, DaemonSets, Jobs and Warning events across all namespaces in six paged single-kind `kubectl get -o json` reads (reading the four controller kinds concurrently) and prints only what needs attention:

   ```bash
   python3 /opt/data/scripts/workload_triage.py
   ```

   Pass `--include-system` on that scan only when the question names a system component or system namespace. It ends with `unhealthy: <count>` (unhealthy pods plus controllers below desired, outside system namespaces) followed by `system unhealthy: <count>` (unhealthy pods plus controllers below desired in folded system namespaces: `kube-system`, `kube-public`, `kube-node-lease`, `gmp-system`, `gmp-public`, `cnrm-system`, `configconnector-operator-system`, `krmapihosting-system`, `istio-system`, `asm-system`, `anthos-identity-service`, `gatekeeper-system`, `composer-system`, `gke-*`, and `config-management-*`; user namespaces ending in `-system` are not folded). Its rules, for reading the objects yourself where the script cannot run: a pod with `deletionTimestamp` set (`Terminating`) or `Pending` behind `SchedulingGated` is initializing regardless of age; a pod whose age (`now - creationTimestamp`) is under 10 minutes with no non-zero container termination in the last 60 minutes is initializing when it is `Pending`, waiting in `ContainerCreating`/`PodInitializing`, or `NotReady`; a pod is unhealthy when a container waits in `CrashLoopBackOff`, `RunContainerError`, an image pull error or a config error, when its age is at least 10 minutes and it is `Pending`, waiting in `ContainerCreating`/`PodInitializing` or `NotReady`, when it is `NotReady`, `PodInitializing` or Ready now after a container restarted in the last 60 minutes (`RecentRestart`), or when its phase is `Failed` unless its owning `Job` has completed, its owning `CronJob` `Job` failed more than 60 minutes ago and a newer sibling `Job` has `Complete=True`, or it ended more than 60 minutes ago and is owned by a `ReplicaSet`, `StatefulSet` or `DaemonSet`; a `Deployment`, `StatefulSet` or `DaemonSet` with ready below desired, condition `ReplicaFailure=True`, or updated below desired alongside a `FailedCreate` warning in the last 60 minutes, or an inactive, incomplete `Job` with condition `Failed=True` or a `FailedCreate` warning in the last 60 minutes (unless older than 60 minutes and superseded by a newer completed sibling `Job` of the same `CronJob`), counts under controllers below desired, with a failed `Job`'s retained pods counted under the `Job` row rather than beside it; Warning events count from the last 60 minutes. Exit code 2 means a cluster read failed or returned truncated or unparseable output: block the card as `needs_input` as [`references/reporting.md`](references/reporting.md) ("Cannot proceed") describes, quoting the exact stderr line.

2. Check `WARNING EVENTS` for any row not already covered by an `UNHEALTHY PODS` or `CONTROLLERS BELOW DESIRED` row: on rows whose `STATE` is `gone` or `-` (the event's object is no longer present or is not a scanned pod or controller — skip rows whose `STATE` is `healthy`, `initializing` or `stale_failed`, except `FailedCreate` on an incomplete `Job`), route `FailedScheduling` to `pod-pending-scheduling`, route `FailedMount`, `ProvisioningFailed` and `FailedAttachVolume` to `pod-container-creating`, and route `FailedCreate` (plus `FailedKillPod` or `Failed` on a `-` controller-kind object such as `ReplicaSet`) to step 7's controller `.status.conditions` (`ReplicaFailure`) and `reason=FailedCreate` check. Because neither `gone` nor `-` has a live pod to read, skip the target skill's step 1 `kubectl get pod`: for a `gone` pod row, start at step 2 (`events` filtered by `involvedObject.name`) and read the owning workload's current pods if needed to confirm whether a replacement is running; for a `-` non-pod row (such as `PersistentVolumeClaim/<name>`), start at step 2 (`events` for that object's name) and the target skill's object-kind step; for a `FailedCreate`, `FailedKillPod` or controller `Failed` row routed to step 7, inspect the owning controller or `Job` and confirm whether a newer revision or pod has converged before reporting a failure. Quote all other `WARNING EVENTS` rows (including `healthy`, `initializing` and `stale_failed` ones) as the event states them without loading a focused skill.

3. If the scan ends with `unhealthy: 0` and `system unhealthy: 0` and step 2 routed no warning event, stop here and run no further command. Report the summary line (pods healthy, initializing, controllers below desired, warning events), any initializing pods by name, any `WARNING EVENTS` rows, the `RESTARTED EARLIER` and `FAILED EARLIER` rows when the question is about restarts or failures, and the system-namespace lines as written (they carry restart counts for system pods). This is a status question with nothing failing, answered in one sentence plus the evidence that settles it — the scan's first line (kubeconfig context and UTC time), its summary line and the command. Re-listing pods, events or controllers to confirm a clean scan adds nothing. If `unhealthy: 0` and `system unhealthy: N` (`N > 0`) and step 2 routed no warning event, stop here and run no further command, but do not report `"No failing workloads."`: report that non-system namespaces have 0 unhealthy workloads while folded namespaces have `N` unhealthy pods or controllers below desired, quoting the summary line and the `SYSTEM NAMESPACES AND NODES` lines as written. When the question did not ask about system namespaces, there is no next step: omit any `[next: ...]` line (never offer or run `--include-system`) and stop your turn after reporting.

4. Otherwise, group the `UNHEALTHY PODS` rows by owning workload (the pod name minus its ReplicaSet or ordinal suffix). Pods of one workload share a cause: investigate one representative pod.

5. Take up to three workloads, ordered by the `STATUS` column `UNHEALTHY PODS` prints: `CrashLoopBackOff`, `RunContainerError`, `Evicted`/`Failed` or `RecentRestart` first (using `LAST EXIT` such as `137 OOMKilled` or `1 Error` to break ties), then `Unschedulable`/`Pending`, then `ImagePullBackOff`/`ErrImagePull`/`InvalidImageName`/`ErrImageNeverPull`, `CreateContainerConfigError`/`CreateContainerError` and `ContainerCreating`/`PodInitializing`, then `NotReady`. Load the skill in the row's `SKILL` column and follow it for the representative pod until the cause is named, usually its first two steps. List any further workloads with their scan row only, and say they were not investigated.

6. `RESTARTED EARLIER` rows (Ready now, last restart before the events window) and `FAILED EARLIER` rows (pods from a completed or `CronJob`-superseded `Job`, or replaced pods that ended before the window) are history. List them when the question asks about restarts or failures; do not investigate them.

7. For a `Job/<name>` row marked `failed: <reason>` in `CONTROLLERS BELOW DESIRED` with no matching `UNHEALTHY PODS` row (a failed `Job` whose pods the controller removed, such as `DeadlineExceeded` or `BackoffLimitExceeded` under `restartPolicy: OnFailure`), inspect `kubectl get job <name> -n <namespace> -o json` and its namespace events (`kubectl get events -n <namespace> --field-selector involvedObject.name=<name>`) to report the cause and proposed fix. For any other controller in `CONTROLLERS BELOW DESIRED` with no matching `UNHEALTHY PODS` row, first check `WARNING EVENTS` for `FailedCreate` in that namespace and, whenever pods are missing (`INITIALIZING` rows do not account for the gap) or `FailedCreate` is present, read the controller's `.status.conditions` (`kubectl get <kind>/<name> -n <namespace> -o jsonpath='{.status.conditions}'` for `ReplicaFailure`) and `kubectl get events -n <namespace> --field-selector reason=FailedCreate`: if `ReplicaFailure` or `FailedCreate` is present, report the quota, admission webhook or policy denial cause and proposed fix; report the controller as rolling out or initializing (with the counts as read, without waiting) only when no creation failure exists.

8. Record the result as [`references/reporting.md`](references/reporting.md) describes.

# Rules

- Keep the scan itself to about six commands, the script included. Each workload step 5 selects adds its focused skill's steps on top of that, usually two commands.
- Report the current state only. Never `sleep`, `--watch`, or re-run the scan to see whether a pod comes up. A pod in `INITIALIZING` is reported as initializing.
- Do not explore folded system namespaces (`kube-system`, `kube-public`, `kube-node-lease`, `gmp-system`, `gmp-public`, `cnrm-system`, `configconnector-operator-system`, `krmapihosting-system`, `istio-system`, `asm-system`, `anthos-identity-service`, `gatekeeper-system`, `composer-system`, `gke-*`, `config-management-*`) or node events. Quote their summary lines as written, including any `(+N more ...)` tail. Pass `--include-system` on step 1's scan only when the question names a system component or system namespace; never run a second scan with `--include-system` after `unhealthy: 0`, and never append `[next: ...]` offering to check system workloads when the question did not ask about system namespaces.
- Do not list pods, nodes or controllers again in other shapes. The scan output is the inventory.
- Do not run `gcloud container clusters get-credentials`. Preflight has already pinned the kubeconfig to this cluster.
